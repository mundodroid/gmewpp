1. The service

Mundo Droid Poster is a Windows desktop application that helps creators organize, schedule and publish their own short videos to their own TikTok account through the official TikTok APIs.

2. User responsibilities

  • You may only publish content that you own or have the rights to publish.
  • You must comply with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Account access

The app connects to TikTok only through the official TikTok login (OAuth). It never asks for your password. You can disconnect your account at any time inside the app or in the TikTok app settings.
